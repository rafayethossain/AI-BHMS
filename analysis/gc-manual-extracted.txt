What does * (Asterix) mean on some GC columns? 
● If you see an Asterix * on any report, it means you can edit directly onto the report. 
● In the Design and Order tabs, you can create and manage the specific areas, and you can 
then manage that process through the reporting tools. 
What is a tick box for  
● A tick box within an order or in a report is there as a verification process. So, if we put a date 
in for an ETD dd/mm/yy with a corresponding tick box, this will mean an unticked box is 
unverified information and therefore more of an estimate. Whereas a tick in the box would 
indicate this information has been verified. This does not mean it won't or can't change, but 
it means it has been confirmed and should happen as per the date. The box should not be 
left unticked if the supplier has confirmed the dates.
